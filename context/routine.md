# My Routine

## Daily shape
- Wake ~9:00. 9:00–10:00 is my getting-ready hour (coffee, scrolling). Don't schedule it unless the day is packed and I agree.
- Work window: 10:00 to 10:30pm.
- Wind down / scroll before bed. Asleep 11pm–12am.
- Lunch and dinner every day (~1 hr each). Lunch ~12:30–1:30, dinner ~6:30–7:30 unless class conflicts.

## Fixed weekly schedule
| Day | Fixed |
|-----|-------|
| Mon | Gym 1.5 hr, afternoon (time flexible) |
| Tue | DBS 5:30–7:30pm, then **extra dinner** after |
| Wed | Systems Programming 2:30–5:20, Gen AI 5:30–8:30, then **extra dinner** after |
| Thu | Gym 1.5 hr, afternoon (time flexible) |
| Fri | Distributed Systems 2:30–5:30, then **extra dinner** after |

On long days (Tue/Wed/Fri), the extra dinner after class is the last meal. On Wed, regular dinner can happen **during Gen AI class** (I sometimes eat in class); the post-class dinner still stays.

## Energy
- **Mornings:** light / busy work. DBS homework fits here.
- **After lunch:** best focus. Put deep work here (exam study, Distributed homework, quiz prep, Gen AI homework).
- **Best days:** Monday and Thursday (wide open), Tuesday afternoon (very productive).
- **Wednesday is tight:** all real work has to happen before 2:30.
